About the Cafe

Big D. Coffee & Tea Hub is a popular neighborhood cafe located in Barangay Banay Banay, City of Cabuyao, Laguna. It serves as a cozy local hangout spot known for its affordable drinks, comfort snacks, and relaxed atmosphere. It is open with varying hours depending on the day.

  • "Happiness starts at Big D."
  • Opening Hours: Mon–Thu, 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM; Fri–Sun, 1:00 PM – 10:00 PM
  • Location: Block 13, Lot 1, Katapatan Homes Mainroad, Brgy. Banay Banay, City of Cabuyao, Laguna
