About the Cafe

San's Cafe is a cozy neighborhood coffee spot and local favorite in the City of Cabuyao, Laguna, with branches including Katapatan Mutual Homes (Banay Banay) and Mamatid.

  • "Where the sun's light meets San's brew, every day begins anew."
  • Opening Hours: Mon–Sun, 10:00 AM – 11:00 PM
  • Location: Block 1, Lot 13, Katapatan Homes, Brgy. Banay Banay, City of Cabuyao, Laguna
