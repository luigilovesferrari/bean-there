How We Rate a Cafe

Every badge on this guide is earned the same way, in this order, every visit.

01

The First Sip

Black coffee first. No sugar, no distractions. We want to taste what the café is really serving.

02

The Wait

Good coffee deserves a little patience. We notice how the café handles the rush without letting quality disappear.

03

The Seat Test

A café should feel worth staying in. We look at the comfort, atmosphere, and whether two hours somehow feels like twenty minutes.

04

The Comeback

Would we return without a reason? No plans, no occasion just a random day and a craving for that same cup. That's the score that matters.
