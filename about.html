About Us

Bean There started with five friends, a shared love for coffee, and plenty of opinions about which cafés were actually worth visiting. Instead of debating over favorites, we decided to visit them ourselves and let every cup speak for itself.

We explore cafés for more than just their coffee. We look at the atmosphere, the comfort, the little details, and the overall experience that makes a place memorable.

No paid favorites. No complicated formulas. Just five friends, honest opinions, and a growing list of cafés worth discovering.
